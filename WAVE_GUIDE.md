# Ten-wave campaign guide

One new tower may be placed per wave, shared between setup and combat. There are ten pads and a maximum of ten towers. Money and upgrades carry between waves.

**Total: 338 enemies — 188 Scouts, 49 Runners, 40 Armored, 38 Motorcycles, 22 Trucks and 1 Siege elite.**

Motorcycles are the fastest enemy at 5.2 units/second and pay $20. Trucks move at 1.05 units/second, have 160 base HP (40 times a base Scout), and pay $120. Every type's HP scales by 12% of base per wave after wave 1. Trucks therefore have 236.8 HP in wave 5 and 332.8 HP in wave 10. Across the entire campaign, monster kills pay $7,115.

Place rapid Pulse towers near bends to cover fast traffic. Taller upgraded Siege towers have greater damage and range, making them useful against convoys. Save some cash for each wave's new placement before committing everything to upgrades. Victory requires clearing the final truck convoy in wave 10; any escape still ends the run.
